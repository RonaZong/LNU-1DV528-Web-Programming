* Meet a hacker, "[Detectify | Meet the Hacker - Fredrik Nordberg Almroth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ERXWb0KjMRo)". A 6 minutes video where we meet a "hacker" who gives us some insight into his everyday business.
